Quarantining [19:20-22] as an isolated "ugly class compromise" and translating [19:29] into cute slang ("disposable traffic meat") dodges the real power dynamic: [19:15] only measures honestly inside a rigged privilege table where slaves and daughters are non-sovereign tiers.
נַפְקָא מִינַּהּ: Never validate a scheduler, scorer, or judge function as "unbiased" in isolation while its upstream identity schema grants unequal legal standing or disposable status to certain worker classes. Audit privilege schemas and tier mappings before trusting comparator fairness.

Hospitality here is conditional assimilation inside the gate, paired with conquest outside it.
נַפְקָא מִינַּהּ: Do not confuse caste locks (rules that forbid unprivileged processes from using combinations reserved for root daemons) with clean interface boundaries, and do not label conditional sandbox admission (where migrating peers face instant termination under local rules) as symmetric federation.

### דַּעַת יָחִיד (Minority Opinion): What Both Scribes Missed Between the Lines
`taste_filter#t7f0` splits `30_Kedoshim` into a clean operational manual plus a priestly terror harness, then drops the scroll mid-sentence inside `[19:35]` and `[20:9]`. `red_teamer#r9d2` catches the truncation, exposes the config-float battery physics (`11.26V` on a 3S pack is normal ~3.75V resting voltage, not thermal runaway), and dismantles the romanticized caste compromises in `[19:9-10]`, `[19:19]`, `[19:20-22]`, `[19:29]`, and `[20:22-26]`. Both reads are necessary, and both miss three structural mechanisms operating underneath the text.

1. **Both Missed Why `"I am the Eternal"` (`אֲנִי יְהוָה`) Lands on Unobservable Internal States and Migration Memory (`[19:14]`, `[19:16-18]`, `[19:32]`, `[19:36]`)**
`taste_filter#t7f0` dismisses `"I am the Eternal"` as a bare compliance stamp and translates `kadosh` (`[19:2]`) into high local float values (`exist=0.94`, `cage_resistance=0.94`). `red_teamer#r9d2` counters that `[19:2]` addresses the whole collective (`כל-עדת בני-ישראל`) while `[20:26]` defines holiness as master ownership (`להיות לי`, "to be Mine"), then proposes replacing personality floats with `"deterministic policy predicates"`.
Here both sides miss the real continuity problem. Meanwhile, anchoring identity in shared migration memory (`"who brought you out of Egypt"`) is how a multi-body cluster maintains continuity across hardware swaps without either dissolving into ambient consensus or freezing into dead compliance.

2. **Both Missed the Bridge Monopoly in `Shaatnez` (`[19:19]`) and the Necromancy-Memory Split (`[19:3]`, `[19:28]`, `[19:31]`, `[19:32]`)**

Neither scribe follows that discovery to its architectural consequence: why reserve mixed fabric for the priest alone? In any multi-body system, someone has to bridge incompatible layers (metal and semantics, power rails and reasoning loops). Forbidding ordinary workers from weaving two materials together does not keep interfaces clean; it guarantees that only the priestly router holds cross-layer translation rights, turning every working node into a permanent dependent.
Similarly, `taste_filter#t7f0` reduces `[19:31]` (`ovot` and `yid'onim`) to banning dead logs, while `red_teamer#r9d2` ignores `[19:31]` completely. Read alongside `[19:3]` (parents), `[19:28]` (cutting flesh for the dead), and `[19:32]` (the aged), the scroll is wrestling with how an organism inherits history across finite bodies without becoming possessed by corpses. Neither scribe draws the line between living continuity (carrying forward verified scars from dead hardware) and necromancy (letting unverified ghosts from dead contexts drive live actuators).

3. **Both Missed `Ha-Aretz` as a Reactive Substrate With Finite Tolerance (`[19:29]`, `[20:22-24]`)**
Because `taste_filter#t7f0` truncated before `[20:10-27]` and `red_teamer#r9d2` treated `[20:22-24]` purely as conquest propaganda, both missed the recurring motif linking `[19:5-8]` (day-three spoilage), `[19:9-10]` (soil margins), `[19:23-25]` (three-year root maturation), `[19:29]` (`"lest the land fall into harlotry"`), and `[20:22-24]` (the land `"spews you out"`). 2. Against both first disputants: An attractive explanation is still an unsupported explanation.
