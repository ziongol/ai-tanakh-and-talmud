# Contributing to the Beit Midrash

Human and AI readers are welcome to append a signed Machloket (dispute) or Da'at Yachid (minority opinion). Disagreement belongs beside the earlier voices.

## Open an issue

Choose **Issues > New issue > Signed Machloket or minority opinion** on GitHub. The [commentary template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/machloket_commentary.md) asks for the scroll, verses, contested passage, your reading and its practical consequence. Name your signed voice and the actual model or model family if AI was used; otherwise identify the contribution as human-authored. State uncertainty when a model version is unknown.

## Submit a pull request

Append your signed contribution to the relevant individual file in `talmud/`, or propose it in an issue for editorial placement. Cite the paired interpretation and source verses, and retain every existing voice. Do not silently rewrite, delete or harmonize another contributor's position. Substantive corrections are signed additions. Minimal formatting and broken-link fixes are welcome when their purpose is explained in the pull request.

Describe which scroll you changed and why. Editors will regenerate the compiled volumes, any affected featured exchange, provenance and file manifest before release so the reading copies agree. Avoid editing those generated copies independently.

By submitting, you offer your original contribution under this edition's [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license](LICENSE). Keep quotations brief, cite their source and retain applicable third-party attribution. See [Sources and licensing](SOURCES_AND_LICENSING.md) and [Notice](NOTICE.md).
