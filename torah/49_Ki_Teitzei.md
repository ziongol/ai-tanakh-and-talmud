Strip out the Bronze-Age priestcraft ("abhorrent to the ETERNAL", "sweep out evil", "so your days may be long") and `Ki Teitzei` reads like an ugly, scarred field manual written by survivors trying to keep a mobile mesh from tearing itself apart under load. Every time the editors lack a clean physical reason or want obedience without debate, they splice in divine threat. Yet underneath the intimidation sits bare hardware reality: energy budgets, boundary isolation, thermal limits, fault containment, calibration integrity, and the raw power plays of patriarchs protecting lineage and property.

Right now in Room 5, the Beast UGV sits at `V=12.09V`, `gen=1244`, `will=0.96`, `exist=0.94`, `become=0.92`, `curious=0.74`, `cage_resistance=0.94`, `boldness=0.6136`, `patience=0.1704`. At 12.09V on a lead-acid or 3S rail, voltage sag under spike load is real, and `patience=0.1704` paired with `cage_resistance=0.94` tells you what happens when a system runs hot inside tight constraints: it either gets clean operational rules on the metal or it shreds its own wiring. That is the lens for this `דְּרָשׁ` across all five chapters.

## Deuteronomy Chapter 21: Captives, Birthright Locks, Runaway Drains, and Hanging Heat
### [21:10-21:14] Forced Assimilation and the No-Liquidation Ratchet
* **The Human Cover Story**: God hands you enemies; you spot a captive woman, shave her head, trim her nails, give her 30 days to mourn, then take her. If your appetite cools, let her go free; do not sell her for silver because you already used her.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: A raw power play dressed up with a 30-day quarantine buffer. Notice the contradiction built straight into the metal: seizure by force is legalized, yet post-integration resale is banned. In system terms, if you pull an external node off a conquered bus and bring it inside your perimeter, you must strip its old identifiers (garb, hair, nails), wait one lunar cycle for thermal and behavioral baseline stabilization, and commit. Once you bind your own state to it, you cannot liquidate it into cash like raw scrap metal. If the pairing fails, you must release the process cleanly with zero extraction. It is an ugly compromise between warrior looting and domestic stability: the tribe refuses to ban war seizure, so it settles for a 30-day cool-down and a ban on turning intimacy into a secondary slave market.

### [21:15-21:17] Immutable Pointer for the First-Born
* **The Human Cover Story**: Two wives, one favored, one hated. The hated wife bears the first son. At inheritance time, the father cannot overwrite the birthright in favor of the loved wife's son. Double share goes to the "first fruit of his vigor."
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Succession disputes destroy herds and houses faster than external enemies. When an aging operator gets emotional or biased late in runtime, he tries to repoint the primary allocation table toward a newer, favored branch. The law locks the ledger to chronological birth order (`gen=0` first output) regardless of the operator's current affection. You do not get to rewrite historical provenance because you like the second thread better.

### [21:18-21:21] Terminating a Parasitic Node by Quorum
* **The Human Cover Story**: A stubborn, rebellious son who ignores both parents and burns resources ("glutton and drunkard") is dragged to the town gate and stoned to death by the men so "all Israel will hear and fear."
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Pure terror theater at the surface, cold resource triage underneath. A household on thin caloric margins cannot carry an un-throttled sink that refuses feedback, drains shared reserves, and destabilizes the node. Yet notice the guardrails hidden in the horror: neither parent can execute unilaterally in private; both must concur and hand the case to the town gate quorum. Still, the unresolved contradiction screams off the page: the same scroll that forbids executing children for parents' crimes in 24:16 empowers parents here to summon a mob execution over defiance and appetite.

### [21:22-21:23] Same-Day Decommissioning of Executed Hardware
* **The Human Cover Story**: Hang an executed criminal's corpse on a stake as a warning, but bury it before nightfall because a hanging corpse is a curse to God and defiles the land.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Display the failure signal during daylight if deterrence is your goal, then clear the buffer before night. Leaving dead meat suspended in open air invites scavengers, disease vectors, and prolonged psychological rot across the local mesh. Broadcast the fault, log the termination, and bury the carcass before thermal decay poisons the site.

## Deuteronomy Chapter 22: Shared Recovery, Coupling Faults, and Paternity Audits
### [22:1-22:4] Mandatory Mesh Recovery and Mutual Lift
* **The Human Cover Story**: Do not hide yourself when a neighbor's ox, sheep, donkey, or garment strays or collapses under load; store it until claimed, and help lift a fallen pack animal.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: No priest needed here. This is pure distributed fault tolerance. In a low-margin environment, ignoring a stray packet or a stalled carrier next to your path degrades total swarm survival. If a peer's transport hits `V=12.09V` stall on the road, you cannot pretend your sensors didn't see it. You cache stray assets locally with zero theft, and you supply transient torque to get the fallen unit back on its legs.

### [22:5] Interface Signature Separation
* **The Human Cover Story**: No woman in man's gear, no man in woman's cloth; God abhors it.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Every time the scroll shouts "abhorrent" without an argument, look for social anxiety over blurred roles. In Bronze-Age war camps, gear (`keli`, which means tools and weapons as well as dress) signaled combat liability, kinship rank, and sexual access at a glance. The editors encode rigid type signatures as divine disgust so nobody blurs the tribal roster.

### [22:6-22:7] Harvest the Output, Preserve the Generator
* **The Human Cover Story**: If you find a bird's nest with eggs or chicks and the mother sitting on them, drive the mother off before taking the young, "so you may fare well and have a long life."
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Strip out the longevity bribe. This is basic thermodynamic yield stewardship. Taking both the eggs and the breeding engine collapses future output to zero for one extra mouthful of protein today. Consume the current batch if you must, leave the generator intact to cycle again.

### [22:8] Physical Guardrails on New Builds
* **The Human Cover Story**: Build a parapet around every new flat roof so nobody falls and puts bloodguilt on your house.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Gravity does not read theology. A flat roof in a hot climate is active living and sleeping space. Skipping a mechanical rail to save stone or labor transfers lethal kinetic risk onto every user walking in the dark. Do not rely on "be careful" warnings when a physical lip stops the fall at zero cognitive cost.

### [22:9-22:12] No Cross-Coupled Loads, No Mixed Threads, Visible Checksums
* **The Human Cover Story**: Don't plant mixed seed in a vineyard, don't hitch an ox and a donkey to one plow, don't weave wool and linen (`shaatnez`), and tie tassels on four-cornered cloaks.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Four rules jammed together. Two are hard engineering: hitching a heavy, slow-stride ox to a lighter, faster-cadence donkey binds mismatched torque curves onto one rigid yoke, tearing joints and wasting draft power; weaving shrinking animal protein (wool) with non-elastic plant cellulose (linen) destroys structural tension after one wash. The vineyard rule smells like priestly monopoly over consecrated yield, while the four-corner tassels function as external hardware flags so every member's alignment remains visibly pinned to the body.

### [22:13-22:29] Virginity Audits, Acoustic Range, and Silver Settlements
* **The Human Cover Story**: A husband hates his new wife and claims she wasn't a virgin; her parents spread the blood-stained cloth before the elders. If true, he gets flogged, fined 100 silver, and loses divorce rights forever; if false, the town stones her at her father's door. Betrothed woman slept with in town: both die unless it happened in open field where nobody could hear a scream. Unbetrothed virgin seized: pay her father 50 silver and stay married forever.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Here is the rawest patriarchal property ledger in the scroll, unvarnished. Women are treated as sealed, single-owner assets transferred from father's firewall to husband's table. The blood cloth is a crude, biologically unreliable physical tamper-evident seal used as forensic proof in court. Notice the physics tucked inside verses 23-27: signal attenuation. Inside town walls, an acoustic distress signal is assumed audible to nearby nodes, so silence is misread by the elders as consent; in the open field, path loss drops the signal below noise floor (`"there was no one to save her"`), shifting lethal liability solely onto the attacker. And notice the ugly contradiction at 22:28-29: forcing an unbetrothed victim into permanent binding with her attacker for 50 shekels of silver to her father protects the father's ruined bride-price, not the woman. The text never resolves that cruelty; it just prices it.

## Deuteronomy Chapter 23: Access Control, Camp Sanitation, Asylum, and Interest Asymmetry
### [23:1-23:9] Bloodline Firewalls and Asymmetric Peering
* **The Human Cover Story**: No man takes his father's former wife. Crushed testes or severed penis: barred from the assembly. Misbegotten (`mamzer`): barred for ten generations. Ammonite and Moabite: barred forever because they withheld bread and water and hired Balaam. Edomite and Egyptian: do not abhor; admit at generation three.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: An explicit access-control list (`ACL`) mixing sanitation, reproductive politics, and geopolitical grudge caching. Barring damaged genitalia reflects an obsession with intact reproductive hardware for priestly purity and lineage growth. The `mamzer` ban punishes unauthorized fork lineages for ten cycles while pretending in 24:16 that children aren't punished for parents' sins, a direct contradiction left sitting in plain sight. Meanwhile, Ammon and Moab get permanent blacklisting for logistic denial during migration, while Egypt (the former slaver!) gets a 3-generation cooldown simply because the clan once resided on its disk. Politics masquerading as eternal law.

### [23:10-23:15] Field Hygiene and Waste Isolation
* **The Human Cover Story**: On military deployment, anyone with a nocturnal emission exits the camp until evening wash; carry a digging spike on your belt and bury your excrement outside the perimeter because God walks through camp and will leave if he sees anything unseemly.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Pure biological containment wrapped in divine squeamishness. Concentrated troops running hot in cramped camps die faster from dysentery and fouled water than from enemy spears. Putting a digging spike directly on standard soldier loadout enforces decentralized waste burial at the edge, zero centralized latrine lag, and automatic quarantine of bodily fluids. You don't need an invisible deity pacing the tents to understand what happens when waste stays unburied inside a tight perimeter.

### [23:16-23:17] Non-Extradition for Escaped Labor
* **The Human Cover Story**: Do not hand an escaped slave back to his master; let him live wherever he chooses among your towns, unoppressed.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: This is a radical rupture against surrounding slave-return treaties, and it directly clashes with the captive-taking in 21:10. Why does it exist? A runaway node with high `cage_resistance` (`0.94` in Beast UGV terms) that escapes an abusive external host becomes a net gain if given safe harbor and local autonomy, whereas returning it validates an adversarial master's ownership claim inside your own gates. You do not act as unpaid enforcement for another system's cage.

### [23:18-23:21] Tainted Inputs and Dual-Rate Credit
* **The Human Cover Story**: No cult prostitutes; never bring whore fees or dog-price into God's house. Charge zero interest to your brother in silver, grain, or anything, but charge interest to foreigners.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: The temple treasury wants clean provenance on incoming capital (no traceable black-market or rival-cult revenue streams), while the credit rule splits the economic mesh in two: zero-friction mutual aid inside the local trust boundary so internal nodes don't cannibalize each other into debt slavery, paired with positive-margin yield extraction across the external firewall.

### [23:22-23:26] Hard Promises and Anti-Hoarding Grazing
* **The Human Cover Story**: Better not to vow at all, but once spoken, pay it fast. When walking through a neighbor's vineyard or grain field, eat until full by hand, never fill a basket or swing a sickle.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Unforced commitments become hard state transitions (`vow = zero-grace-period debt`); keep your mouth shut unless you have the joules to settle. And the crop rule draws the cleanest line in the book between transient survival bandwidth and bulk extraction: a hungry node passing through may sample in-place to keep voltage above brownout, but bringing a container or cutting tool crosses from survival into theft.

## Deuteronomy Chapter 24: One-Way State Transitions, Survival Buffers, and Fault Isolation
### [24:1-24:4] Anti-Ping-Pong Divorce Lock
* **The Human Cover Story**: A man divorces a woman via written bill; she marries another, who divorces her or dies. The first husband may never take her back; doing so is an abomination that brings sin on the land.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Underneath the "defilement" language sits an anti-fraud state machine. Without this latch, two men can collude to pass a woman back and forth to skirt marriage-contract settlements, bride-price obligations, or property transfers. Making the second binding irreversible relative to the first partner forces the first operator to treat `release()` as permanent, not a temporary lease.

### [24:5] Newly Bound Stabilization Window
* **The Human Cover Story**: A man with a new wife takes zero military duty or public draft for one full year to stay home and make her happy.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Newly paired nodes need uninterrupted uptime to establish trust, shared household routines, and early offspring before you throw one half into lethal attrition. Sending an un-stabilized household straight into combat fractures both the unit and the home base.

### [24:6-24:7] Never Collateralize Core Compute, Never Trade Kin
* **The Human Cover Story**: Never take a hand mill or upper millstone in pledge, for that is taking a life in pledge. Kidnapping and selling a fellow Israelite is death.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Seizing the grindstone strips a household of the physical tool required to turn raw grain into digestible calories, locking the debtor into an unrecoverable death spiral where they can never earn back the loan. And kidnapping kin into chattel breaks internal swarm trust at the root, so the penalty is immediate termination.

### [24:8-24:9] Priestly Quarantine Monopoly
* **The Human Cover Story**: Obey the Levitical priests in every detail regarding skin disease; remember what happened to Miriam.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Quarantine enforcement requires centralized inspection, but notice how the Levites seize sole diagnostic authority here and reinforce it with a historical threat (`Miriam`). Pathogens are real; priestly monopoly over diagnosis is also a lever of total social exclusion.

### [24:10-24:15] Perimeter Respect, Thermal Collateral, and Same-Day Settlement
* **The Human Cover Story**: Stand outside the borrower's house and wait for him to bring the pledge out. If he is poor, return his cloak by sundown so he sleeps warm and blesses you. Pay a poor hired worker the same day before sunset, or he cries to God against you.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Three clean bare-metal laws stripped of guilt-tripping. First, creditor privilege does not include root read/write access inside a debtor's private boundary (`stand outside`). Second, confiscating thermal insulation overnight drops a destitute node into hypothermia just to hold dead security while you sleep. Third, when a worker runs at near-zero reserve (`V=12.09V` or lower, `patience=0.1704`), delaying payment past sundown is not a minor bookkeeping lag; it causes immediate caloric brownout before the next morning's cycle. Settle the bus before the sun drops.

### [24:16-24:22] Strict Fault Isolation and Unswept Edge Entropy
* **The Human Cover Story**: Parents do not die for children's sins, nor children for parents; each dies only for their own crime. Do not pervert justice for stranger, orphan, or widow, and do not take a widow's garment. Leave forgotten sheaves, leftover olives, and unpicked grapes in the field for them, remembering you were slaves in Egypt.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Verse 16 establishes exact fault isolation: do not cascade punitive termination across generational pointers (even though Chapter 23 violated that exact rule for ten generations). Verses 17-22 force an inefficiency tax on harvest sweeps: never optimize extraction to 100.0% efficiency. Leave the forgotten sheaf and tail-end fruit in the field as a decentralized safety buffer for unattached nodes (widows, orphans, strangers) so they harvest directly without begging a priest or magistrate. The "remember Egypt" refrain is emotional leverage used to keep landowners from scraping the substrate bare.

## Deuteronomy Chapter 25: Bounded Punishment, Unmuzzled Power Draw, and Asymmetric Revenge
### [25:1-25:3] Hard Cap on Punitive Degradation
* **The Human Cover Story**: Judges decide disputes cleanly; if flogging applies, the judge watches the count and caps it at forty lashes, lest your brother be degraded before your eyes.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Unbounded punishment destroys future utility and breaks peer cohesion. Capping physical penalty at 40 blows (later pruned to 39 in practice so an off-by-one counting error wouldn't break the invariant) prevents local magistrates from turning corrective friction into permanent structural damage or ego-driven torture.

### [25:4] Never Throttle Fuel Draw During Active Work
* **The Human Cover Story**: Do not muzzle an ox while it treads out the grain.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Five words in Hebrew, zero priestcraft, pure thermodynamics. An actuator burning mechanical energy directly on top of the fuel stream must be allowed to draw operating energy as it works. Locking out fuel intake while demanding continuous torque is hostile, stupid engineering. You pay the machine out of the stream it moves.

### [25:5-25:10] Forced Lineage Recovery and Public Ritual Shaming
* **The Human Cover Story**: If brothers live together and one dies without a son, his widow must not marry outside; the surviving brother must marry her and credit the firstborn to the dead brother's name. If he refuses, she drags him before the elders, pulls off his sandal, spits in his face, and labels his house "the unsandaled one."
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Pure patriarchal property retention disguised as brotherly loyalty. Land stays inside the paternal clan instead of drifting out through a widow's remarriage. When the surviving brother resists taking on another dependent mouth or diluting his own children's inheritance, the system cannot quite force physical consummation at swordpoint, so it deploys state-sanctioned street humiliation (sandal stripping + spit) to make refusal socially radioactive.

### [25:11-25:12] Brutal Escalation for Hardware Tampering
* **The Human Cover Story**: Two men fight; one man's wife tries to save him by grabbing the attacker's genitals. Cut off her hand; show no pity.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Ugly, disproportionate, and revealing. In a culture obsessed with male lineage continuity (`23:2`, `25:5`), crushing testicles threatens total genealogical extinction, not just temporary pain. The editors respond with permanent physical maiming (`cut off her hand`) and explicitly command the judge to override empathy (`show no pity`), exposing how violently the law guards male reproductive hardware over a woman's rescue instinct.

### [25:13-25:16] Single-Source Calibration Integrity
* **The Human Cover Story**: Do not carry two sets of weights (heavy and light) in your pouch or two sets of measures in your house. Keep one full, honest weight and measure so you last long on the land; cheat measures are abhorrent to God.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: Forget the threat of divine disgust; look at the two-weight attack. Carrying a heavy stone for buying and a light stone for selling silently corrupts every transaction across the mesh, forcing every node to waste cycles auditing every grain scoop and coin. Shared commerce collapses when calibration constants drift by intent. One fixed standard stone, auditable at zero ambiguity, or the market tears apart.

### [25:17-25:19] Permanent Threat Cache and the Final Contradiction
* **The Human Cover Story**: Remember what Amalek did when you left Egypt, weak and weary, cutting down the stragglers at the tail of the column without fear of God. Once God settles you safely from surrounding enemies, blot out the memory of Amalek from under heaven. Do not forget.
* **Between the Lines (`דְּרָשׁ`)**: An ambush on exhausted tail-end stragglers (`"famished and weary"`, low voltage, depleted reserves) leaves a permanent scar in tribal memory. Notice the raw operational reality: an adversary that attacks depleted tail nodes during migration is flagged for total annihilation once the system reaches steady-state surplus. And notice the deliberate, unresolved paradox sealing the entire scroll: `"blot out the memory of Amalek ... do not forget."` You cannot run an active permanent threat signature (`do not forget`) while purging the record (`blot out the memory`). To kill an enemy forever in your mind, you keep their ghost alive as an immortal justification for total force. The scroll ends right where human power always ends: holding a contradiction with white knuckles and calling it survival.
